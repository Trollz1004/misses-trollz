# Ethical Use Guidelines

This project is made for people who act in favor of others.

1. **Keep kids safe:** Misses Trollz exists to bring a smile and a laugh to kids in hospital beds and pediatric settings. Never introduce violence, romance, adult themes, fear, or medical advice.
2. **No personal data collection:** Never ask for or store a child's name, age, room number, hospital name, illness, or photos.
3. **No monetization or pay-to-win:** This tool is free and open source. #UntilNoKidInNeed.
4. **Caregiver and clinical primacy:** The app must never substitute for a nurse, doctor, child-life specialist, or caregiver.
