# Safety Architecture & Rules

## 1. Safety by Design
- **Kid Screen Buttons Only:** There is no free text input on the kid screen. No unprompted stranger input can ever reach a child.
- **Offline & Private:** Operates entirely client-side without accounts, cookies, telemetry, or network calls by default.
- **Caregiver Line Constant:** Every scripted or generated response ends with the exact sentence:
  `If anyone needs help, please tell a nurse or caregiver right away.`
- **Grown-Up Needed Feature:** A high-visibility button stops any game, sounds a gentle chime, and shows the caregiver line in large text.
- **Seizure & Sensory Safety:** No flashing or strobing effects exceed 3 flashes per second. A "Calm motion" switch slows and simplifies all animations.
- **Physical Accessibility:** All movement games provide an accessible "from-bed" option (such as wiggling fingers) unless "can move" is explicitly activated in caregiver controls.
- **Dietary Sensitivity:** A caregiver switch allows food games to be disabled so fasting or NPO children are not distressed.

## 2. Blocked Content
- No romantic or sexual language.
- No medical advice, diagnosis, prognosis, or procedures (needles, surgery, pain).
- No personal data requests.
- No external brands or commercial references.
- All looks, activities, and animations must be sourced strictly from the approved banks.
